Description

A licensed minicab, booked in a few taps, anywhere in London.

Know the price before you go. The fare appears before you confirm and does not change because the traffic did. No meter, no guessing at the end of the journey.

Know who is collecting you. Every Galeyr driver has a code. You get the code, the registration and the driver's name before the car arrives — and anyone can check a code without an account.

Licensed and insured, every time. Private hire licence, hire-and-reward insurance, MOT and right to work are verified before a driver takes a single job, and re-checked as they expire.

Airports, done properly. Heathrow, Gatwick, Stansted, Luton and London City, saved for one tap. We track the flight, so a delayed landing is not a missed car.

Or just ring us. The control room is staffed 24 hours a day, every day of the year, including Christmas.

Driving for Galeyr? Go online when it suits you, see the fare before you accept, and get paid weekly.
